Welcome to my corner of the internet · Data, creativity & everything in between

Making sense of data, together.

Hi! I'm Magda - a data enthusiast based in the United Kingdom, passionate about data and reporting.

You'll find my articles over at bronowska.com, my projects on GitHub, and my videos on the PowerDataHub YouTube channel. I love the community side of data as much as the technical side.

Beyond my own projects, I co-lead the Southampton Data Platform and Cloud User Group, where I handle marketing, speaker selection, and sponsor communications. I also volunteer as a helper at SQL Bits, one of the UK's biggest data conferences.

And when I finally close the laptop - there's LEGO to build, yarn to crochet, places to explore, and something delicious to bake. Life is better with a good mix of all of the above.
